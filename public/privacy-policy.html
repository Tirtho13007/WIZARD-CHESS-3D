Privacy Policy — Wizard Chess 3D

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 • Application: Wizard Chess 3D: Sanctum Edition

Wizard Chess 3D: Sanctum Edition ("the Application") is an interactive 3D chess game that runs locally in your browser or as an installed Android Progressive Web App (PWA / Trusted Web Activity).

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, personally identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, location data, or contact information. All chess gameplay, AI calculations, camera preferences, and 3D rendering happen 100% locally on your device.

2. Local Storage & Offline Caching

The Application uses standard browser Service Worker caching and local storage solely to store 3D models, textures, and application assets on your device so the game loads quickly and works offline without an internet connection.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

The Application contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no user tracking scripts, and no in-app purchases.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the Application does not collect any personal information from any user, it is safe for players of all ages and complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store store listing page.
